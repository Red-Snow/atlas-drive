# Atlas Drive

Open-world 3D street racer that runs in your phone's browser. Pick a real place and the game downloads its streets and buildings from OpenStreetMap, or tap **Drive my street** to race around your own neighbourhood.

**Play:** https://red-snow.github.io/atlas-drive/

- Touch controls (steer, gas, brake, drift) or keyboard (arrows/WASD, Space to drift, R to respawn)
- Checkpoint runs with best times, or free roam with drift scoring
- Golden hour and night modes

Credits: map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Car model by vicent091036 (Sketchfab), from the three.js examples. Built with three.js.
